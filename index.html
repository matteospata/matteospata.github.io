About me

Nice to meet you, I'm Matteo Spata. I was born in Ragusa, Sicily, on September 5, 2002.

Ever since I was a child, I've always been a lively, curious boy with a passion for anything that allowed me to discover and understand the world around me. I've cultivated a wide range of interests: on the one hand, technology and a passion for computers, driven by a curiosity to understand how they worked and to experiment on my own; on the other hand, sports and reading, activities that have been a constant part of my life as I've grown up.

At age 14, I began my studies at the Liceo Classico Umberto I in Ragusa, initially choosing a course of study that I thought would lead me toward a career primarily in the humanities. Over time, however, I realized that the subjects in which I was best able to express my curiosity and abilities were primarily mathematics and physics. This realization gradually began to steer my choices toward a more scientific and quantitative path.

At age 18, I enrolled on the Economics Bachelor's Degree program at the University of Catania, where I developed a strong interest in statistics and data analysis. I soon realized that what fascinated me most was the ability to use data to understand complex phenomena, identify patterns, and transform large amounts of information into useful knowledge.

Driven by this interest, I decided to continue my education by enrolling in the Master's Degree program in Data Science at the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Catania. This program allowed me to acquire skills in numerous fields, including Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Big Data Analytics, Data Mining, Cloud Computing, Time Series, and Network Science. It was an extremely formative period, not only from a technical standpoint but also on a personal level: it allowed me to gain a clearer understanding of what truly inspires me and the professional path I wish to pursue.

During my undergraduate studies, I also had the opportunity to hold various leadership and representative roles at the University of Catania. I was part of the technical and administrative staff of the Department of Economics and Business, an experience that allowed me to gain a deeper understanding of how the university operates and to develop organizational and interpersonal skills. Later, during my master's program, I served as the student representative for the Master's Degree Program in Data Science and for the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science. This role gave me the chance to represent students' needs and concerns, engaging directly with faculty, university staff, and other representatives. I believe these experiences have contributed significantly to my personal growth, helping me develop a sense of responsibility, listening skills, communication skills, and a propensity for teamwork.

During my university studies, I also had the opportunity to complete an internship at the National Institute of Geophysics and Volcanology (INGV). In this role, I analyzed time series data from volcanic sensors with the goal of developing models based on deep learning techniques to predict the onset of paroxysmal phenomena on Mount Etna. This experience allowed me to work with real-world data—complex and characterized by high variability—and to apply the knowledge I had acquired during my academic studies.

I completed my master's degree with a grade of 110 cum laude. My thesis, titled "Optimization Methods in the Inverse Eigenvector Centrality Problem," also received significant academic recognition and was published in the volume Optimization Under Uncertainty: Methods and Applications, published by CRC Press and edited by A. Daniilidis, A. A. Khan, and C. Tammer.

I currently work at Ricca IT as part of the Data Science, Mining & AI group of ASP Ragusa where I am responsible for designing and developing end-to-end data pipelines (ETL/ELT) for processing medical records, integrating machine learning and deep learning techniques to extract actionable and valuable insights from the data. This experience allows me to tackle real-world problems on a daily basis, where the technological aspect is combined with the need for a deep understanding of the application context.

Alongside my academic and professional career, I have always tried to devote part of my time to volunteer work and social engagement. I am a blood donor volunteer with AVIS and ADMO, activities I consider a concrete way to contribute to the well-being of the community and help those in need. I believe these experiences are an important part of who I am, because I believe that personal and professional growth must also be accompanied by a desire to contribute positively to society.

Outside of work and school, I love reading comic books, practicing martial arts, walking my dog, and continuing to study and experiment in the field of Artificial Intelligence. In fact, I consider lifelong learning a fundamental part of my journey: AI is a constantly evolving field, and it is precisely this ongoing transformation that I find most stimulating.

Looking ahead, my goal is to continue growing as a professional in the fields of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, tackling increasingly complex problems and turning my curiosity, my education, and my passion for data into concrete and innovative solutions.
